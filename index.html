CampUSA Program

The InterExchange Camp USA program allows youth to spend 9-15 weeks at an American summer camp. A unique opportunity to work as a counselor, earn a stipend, develop lifelong friendships, and experience true cultural exchange.

Your Role as a Camp Counselor

Camp counselors are the heart of American summer camps. You guide, support, and inspire campers while enjoying an unforgettable cultural exchange experience. Duties include daily supervision, activity leadership, and ensuring health and safety.

🎨 Activity Specialists

Lead sports, arts, outdoor adventures, STEM, or waterfront activities using your specific skills.

🎒 General Counselor

Supervise a cabin group, support daily routines, help with inclusion, and participate in special events.

Earnings and Benefits

  • Stipend: Minimum $2300 up to $3200 USD Pre-tax for 9 weeks. (Extra pay for extra days).
  • Included: Free room and board while working.
  • Insurance: Accident and sickness insurance for the entire duration of the program.
  • Visa & Travel: J1 visa sponsorship (DS2019 Form) and 30-day travel period after camp.

Eligibility & Our Model

  • Between 18 and 28 years of age.
  • Strong English speaker genuinely interested in working with children outdoors.
  • Available from mid-May through late August.
4-Step Model:
Step 1 Selection and Interview (Profile evaluation)
Step 2 Visa and Travel Support (DS-2019 aid)
Step 3 Pre-Departure Orientation
Step 4 Ongoing Support (24/7 assistance in US)

Our Experience

Our J-1 program experience began in 2018. We established connections with several U.S. camps, maintaining professional relationships with directors at Camp Watitoh, Point O'Pines, Camp Mah-Kee-Nac, and Camp Greylock. Our Ankara office grants direct access for quick bureaucratic resolutions. Additionally, from 2019-2025, we successfully managed EU programs (European Solidarity Corps), sending over 40 youth abroad, giving us solid expertise in mobility logistics and intercultural training.
